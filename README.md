# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

> "Friends don't lie."

### Overview
**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi drama series about a group of young friends in the 1980s who witness supernatural forces and secret government exploits in their small town.

### Why I Like It
* Engaging story and mysterious plot
* Great 80s soundtrack and nostalgic aesthetic
* Well-developed characters and strong friendships

### Main Characters
1. Eleven
2. Mike Wheeler
3. Dustin Henderson
4. Lucas Sinclair
5. Will Byers
